# Third-party notices

The packages of ReboFiles carry no code of others: what they use from npm
(`@rebofiles/core`, and `@rebodocs/core` for the previews of Word documents,
which is optional) is installed by npm beside them, with its own licence.

The icons of `@rebofiles/react` are drawn in the manner of Lucide
(https://lucide.dev, ISC licence); their paths are written here.
